01 · MACHINE LEARNING

Which crop might be growing here?

A Random Forest model combines satellite features to estimate one of eight crop and land-cover categories. Results need independent validation.

Illustration · Not a prediction map
02 · SATELLITE INDICES

How has vegetation changed?

NDVI and NDMI help reveal greenness and canopy-moisture changes between available satellite observations. They do not diagnose a specific disease.

Illustration · Not a prediction map
03 · RISK SCREENING

Where should field teams investigate?

Rainfall deficits, possible inundation and seasonal anomalies can prioritize inspection. An alert is a screening signal, not a confirmed loss.

Why can the live satellite dashboard take a moment to open?

The web introduction loads quickly, but the Earth Engine app may need time to fetch imagery and process analysis layers. Wait for the map, then request the layer you want. On mobile or a slow network, this can take longer.
